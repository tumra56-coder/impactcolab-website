About The Decode Studio

Build a strategy your whole team owns.

The Decode Studio is an AI-powered strategy planning tool built for startups, NGOs and mission-driven organisations.

It guides your team through a structured four-step process that surfaces your collective wisdom and turns it into a clear, shared strategy your whole organisation can own and act on. Our philosophy is clear: the intelligence lives in your team, your partners, and the communities you serve. The Decode Studio simply draws it out.

How it works

Emerge from the Decode Studio with unified purpose and a strategy your team and partners own. Your Decode Map is your blueprint for impact, underpinning all aspects of your operations, from your communications to your fundraising, from your design to your impact strategy.

Use it independently, guided by Decode AI. Or book a facilitated session. Either way, the intelligence at the centre is always yours.

How it works

Four steps. One compass.

Each step is guided by Decode AI, which draws out your team's collective thinking, structures it against our bespoke frameworks, and builds it into something clear, actionable and owned by your whole organisation.

01

Think Lab

Your team explores your vision, your values, and the change you want to see in the world. Guided by Decode AI, your collective thinking is surfaced, structured and synthesised into the foundations of your strategy.

02

Change Map

Connect your activities to the change you want to see. Map the journey from what you do to the difference it makes, building a shared understanding of how your work drives impact across your whole team.

03

Smart Outcomes

Decode AI synthesises your team's inputs into clear, trackable outcomes across six domains of change, using our proprietary REACHS framework. A robust outcomes framework your whole organisation can measure against.

04

Decode Map

Decode AI generates your Decode Map — capturing your shared strategy, outcomes and organisational language. The unifying foundation for your communications, funding applications, design thinking and impact strategy.
