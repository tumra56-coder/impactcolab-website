How it works

Four steps.
One shared compass.

Each step is guided by Decode AI, which draws out your team's collective thinking, structures it against our bespoke frameworks, and builds it into something clear, actionable and owned by your whole organisation.

Step 1

Think Lab

Your team explores your vision, your values, and the change you want to see in the world. Guided by Decode AI, your collective thinking is surfaced, structured and synthesised into the foundations of your strategy. No jargon, no templates — just your own intelligence, decoded.

Step 2

Change Map

Connect your activities to the change you want to see. Map the journey from what you do to the difference it makes, building a shared understanding of how your work drives impact across your whole team. A living picture of your pathway to change.

Step 3

Smart Outcomes

Decode AI synthesises your team's inputs into clear, trackable outcomes across six domains of change, using our proprietary REACHS framework. The result is a robust, evidence-ready outcomes framework that your whole organisation can work towards and measure against.

Step 4

Impact Blueprint

Decode AI generates your Impact Blueprint, a complete document capturing your shared strategy, outcomes and organisational language. The unifying foundation for your communications, funding applications, design thinking and impact strategy. One document. Everything that follows.
