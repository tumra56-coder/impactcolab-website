decodeCoLab

Decoding complexity
into clarity.

decodeCoLab is a consultancy for any organisation with a vision to make meaningful change in the world. We believe that without a shared strategy, a reflective practice and a living data and evidence system, impact is difficult to realise, difficult to prove and difficult to communicate.

To help you achieve this, we built two AI-powered tools that harness the intelligence already rooted in and around your organisation, in your teams, your partners, and the communities you serve. It is their insight that powers impact.

The Decode Studio draws it out into a shared strategy. The CoLab grows it into a reflective practice, and a living data and evidence system that helps you adapt, learn, and communicate your story of change.

Our philosophy is simple: the intelligence needed to drive your impact already exists inside your organisation. We just help you decode it.

Start with The Decode Studio now to build a shared vision and strategy your whole team owns. Or dive straight into The CoLab and begin building your integrated impact system today.

Meet Decode AI

Your AI strategic thinker.

Decode AI has undergone rigorous training to support the growth of your organisation across strategy, organisational development, impact measurement and learning systems. Trained in our proprietary REACHS framework, it knows how to ask the right questions, surface assumptions, and turn collective thinking into something clear, structured and actionable.

But Decode AI is not here to replace your thinking. It is here to amplify it. Every insight, every outcome, every strategy starts with you and your team. Your AI strategic thinker simply draws it out, organises it, and builds it into something your whole organisation can own and act on. Decode AI powers every tool in the decodeCoLab ecosystem, from your first strategy session in The Decode Studio to your living learning and evidence system in The CoLab.

Whether you are a startup or an established organisation ready to grow your impact, I meet you where you are. I help you get clear on your purpose, build a strategy your whole team believes in, and create the foundations that unlock all that follows.
I help you develop a robust evaluation framework that supports the collection and analysis of your data, connecting your outcomes to the evidence you gather so your organisation can learn, adapt and communicate the difference you make.
I also help embed a reflective practice into your organisation, creating space to pause, learn and adapt as your work evolves. And when your strategy is built I don't disappear. I help you spot patterns in your evidence and turn your data into stories that matter.

Who I Am

I'm Bouchra Atkinson, an evaluator and facilitator with over a decade of experience helping organisations build systems to embed impact and communicate the change they are making in the world, from governance to community development, from sustainable development to health and social care.

I moved to Malawi from Scotland and witnessed the same challenge experienced everywhere: brilliant strategies, dedicated teams, and extraordinary work, but no simple systems to capture insight, create feedback loops, or collect evidence that anyone could contribute to and use. So I built one. Without a development team or technical background, I decoded the CoLab into existence.
