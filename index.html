Decoding complexity

decodeCoLab is a consultancy that works with any organisation with a vision to make meaningful change in the world. We believe you cannot have impact without a shared vision and a clear strategy. We built The Decode Lab to help your team reach a shared understanding of how change happens and how progress can be made towards it — because without this, even the best-resourced organisations lose direction.

Luckily, most organisations are already surrounded by their most precious assets — their teams, their partners, their collaborators, and the communities they serve. It is their insight that powers impact. We are simply here to help you decode it.

Our philosophy is simple: the intelligence needed to drive your impact already exists inside your organisation. The Decode Lab surfaces it. The CoLab grows it — a holistic, integrated system that takes you through the entire journey of change, synthesising your team's collective knowledge into a shared strategy, a reflective practice, and a living data and evidence system that drives your story forward.

Both tools can be used independently — guided by Decode AI, our AI strategic thinker, at your own pace and in your own time. Or work with me directly to facilitate your team workshops using The CoLab and help you build your strategy for change. Either way, the intelligence at the centre is always yours.

The Decode Lab

Decode helps you build a shared vision for change with your team, map your pathway to impact, and provide the unifying foundations for everything that follows — your communications, your fundraising, your design and impact strategy. Four steps. One shared compass.

Step 1

Think Lab

Your team explores your vision, your values, and the change you want to see in the world. Guided by Decode AI, your collective thinking is surfaced, structured and synthesised into the foundations of your strategy.

Step 2

Change Map

Connect your activities to the change you want to see. Map the journey from what you do to the difference it makes — building a shared understanding of how your work drives impact across your whole team.

Step 3

Smart Outcomes

Decode AI synthesises your team's inputs into clear, trackable outcomes across six domains of change — giving you a robust, evidence-ready framework that your whole organisation can work towards and measure against.

Step 4

Impact Blueprint

Decode AI generates your Impact Blueprint — a complete document capturing your shared strategy, outcomes and organisational language for your communications, funding applications, design thinking and impact strategy.

The Platform

The CoLab

Where human intelligence meets AI.

Watch a demo

The CoLab brings your team together to explore the complexity of your work — combining collective wisdom, lived experience, and diverse perspectives into a shared understanding of how change happens. Guided by Decode, your thinking is synthesised into a clear, structured pathway to impact that your whole organisation owns.

That pathway automatically connects to how you collect evidence. Most of the evidence you need already exists — in emails, WhatsApp messages, field observations, partner reports, and your own reflections. Decode helps you turn that wealth of formal and informal data into a living evidence system that helps you respond, learn and adapt. Log observations, record reflections, tag evidence to your outcomes. Decode does the rest — connecting the pieces and turning them into powerful stories of change.

Use The CoLab independently or with a facilitator guiding the process. Either way, the result is a living theory of change — grounded in your own thinking, growing as your work does.

E

Who I Am

I'm Bouchra Atkinson — an evaluator and facilitator with over a decade of experience working with organisations striving to make meaningful change in the world — from governance to community development, from sustainable development to health and social care.

I moved to Malawi from Scotland and here I witnessed all the same challenges that every organisation I have ever worked with experienced. Despite brilliant theories of change, carefully crafted strategies and dedicated staff doing extraordinary work, they were still struggling to evidence the difference they were making — and to use that evidence to direct their programmes, adapt in real time, and drive impact. All because they lacked simple systems to capture insights, create feedback loops, and collect evidence that anyone, from a frontline worker to a MEL expert, could both contribute to and use.

So I built one — without a development team or a technical background. I held myself to what I believe: that a collaborative approach, a clear framework, and embedding learning in practice are key. With AI as a genuine partner and a methodology honed from over a decade of helping organisations express and evidence their work, I decoded the CoLab into existence.

My mission is to help anyone seeking to make a difference learn, adapt, and implement with impact, using the extraordinary technology we now have at our disposal.
