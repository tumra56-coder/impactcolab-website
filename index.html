Decoding complexity

decodeCoLab is a consultancy that works with any organisation with a vision to make meaningful change in the world. We believe you cannot have impact without a shared vision and a clear strategy across your organisation. We built decodeCoLab to help your team reach a shared understanding of how change happens and how progress can be made towards it because without this, even the most mission-focused organisations lose direction.

Luckily, most organisations are already surrounded by their most precious assets — their teams, their partners, their collaborators, and the communities they serve. It is their insight that powers impact. We are simply here to help you decode it.

Our philosophy is simple: the intelligence needed to drive your impact already exists inside your organisation. The Decode Studio draws it out. The CoLab grows it. A holistic, integrated system that takes you through the entire journey of change, synthesising your team's collective knowledge into a shared strategy, a reflective practice, and a living data and evidence system that drives your story forward.

Both tools can be used independently — both guided by Decode AI, our AI strategic thinker, at your own pace and in your own time. Or work with me directly to facilitate your team workshops using The CoLab and help you build your strategy for change. Either way, the intelligence at the centre is always yours.

Meet Decode AI

Your AI strategic thinker.

Decode AI has undergone rigorous training to support the growth of your organisation across strategy, organisational development, impact measurement, and learning systems. It knows how to ask the right questions, surface assumptions for your team to consider, and turn collective thinking into something clear, structured and actionable.

It is also trained in our own proprietary framework — REACHS — which identifies and tracks change across six domains: Relationships, Engagement, Actions & Behaviour, Collaboration, Habits & Practice, and System Changes. This gives Decode AI a uniquely structured lens for understanding how real change happens in organisations and communities.

But Decode AI is not here to replace your thinking. It is here to amplify it. Every insight, every outcome, every strategy starts with you and your team. Decode AI draws it out, organises it, and builds it into something your whole organisation can own and act on. It powers every tool in the decodeCoLab ecosystem, from your first strategy session in The Decode Studio to your living evidence system in The CoLab.

Who I Am

I'm Bouchra Atkinson, an evaluator and facilitator with over a decade of experience working with organisations striving to make meaningful change in the world, from governance to community development, from sustainable development to health and social care.

I moved to Malawi from Scotland and here I witnessed all the same challenges that every organisation I have ever worked with experienced. Despite brilliant theories of change, carefully crafted strategies and dedicated staff doing extraordinary work, they were still struggling to evidence the difference they were making and to use that evidence to direct their programmes, adapt in real time, and drive impact. All because they lacked simple systems to capture insights, create feedback loops, and collect evidence that anyone, from a frontline worker to a MEL expert, could both contribute to and use.

So I built one, without a development team or a technical background. I held myself to what I believe: that a collaborative approach, a clear framework, and embedding learning in practice are key. With AI as a genuine partner and a methodology honed from over a decade of helping organisations express and evidence their work, I decoded the CoLab into existence.

My mission is to help anyone seeking to make a difference learn, adapt, and implement with impact, using the extraordinary technology we now have at our disposal.
