About The CoLab

One system. The entire journey of change.

The CoLab is a holistic, intelligent platform for organisations serious about impact. It brings together three interconnected Labs — The Decode Studio for strategy, Evaluation Lab for evidence, and Story Lab for reflection and learning — in one complete, connected system.

Most organisations plan in silos. Strategy happens in one place, monitoring and evaluation in another, learning and reporting somewhere else entirely. The CoLab connects all three, so that your strategy directly informs how you collect evidence, your evidence feeds your reflection, and your learning shapes your next chapter.

Guided by Decode AI throughout, The CoLab grows with your organisation — embedding impact thinking into everything you do and turning your collective intelligence into stories that matter.

How it works

Three Labs.
One connected journey.

The CoLab connects strategy, evidence and learning so that each part of your impact journey feeds the next. Start with The Decode Studio and build outward — or enter at any point that fits where your organisation is right now.

The Decode Studio

Build your strategy.

Your entry point into The CoLab. Guided by Decode AI, your team builds a shared vision, maps your pathway to impact and generates your Impact Blueprint — the strategic foundation for everything that follows. Four steps. One shared compass.

Evaluation Lab

Collect your evidence.

Your outcomes from The Decode Studio automatically connect to Evaluation Lab, where you build a practical evidence framework, collect data, and track progress against your outcomes. Log observations, field notes, partner reports and reflections — formal and informal, planned and unexpected.

Story Lab

Tell your story.

Decode AI connects the pieces of your evidence and turns them into powerful stories of change. Story Lab embeds a reflective practice into your organisation — creating space to pause, learn and adapt as your work evolves, and generating narratives that communicate your impact to funders, partners and the communities you serve.

Your AI strategic thinker is with you for the whole journey.

Decode AI has been trained in strategy, organisational development, impact measurement, learning systems and our own proprietary REACHS framework. It is present at every step of The CoLab — from your first strategy session to the evidence you collect and the stories you tell.
